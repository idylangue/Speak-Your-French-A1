Museum Guide

Musée de la Migration · Brussels, a cosmopolitan city

Before you go

Brussels is a multicultural, cosmopolitan city — full of diversity. But concretely, what does that mean? Living together, or segregation? Open your eyes and your ears at the Migration Museum.

🧭 Think about it

The different types of migration

At the museum, pay attention to the different migration profiles. Which words go with which profile?

  • Socio-economic migrant
  • Refugee
  • Expat
  • Migrant
What do they have in common?

Learning the language · finding housing · cultural adaptation · intercultural challenges

What is different?

Social status · access to economic resources
